Frequently asked questions

Good to know

What does the ticket include?

The $45 ticket covers Saturday evening’s dinner and reunion celebration at DiBella’s. The main reunion begins at CDO at 5 PM with a tour and class pictures, followed by dinner at DiBella’s. Friday at Hotel Congress and Saturday’s noon family picnic are separate weekend activities.

Can I bring a guest?

Yes. Purchase one ticket per person attending the Saturday evening event and enter the full name of every guest during registration.

Will I receive confirmation?

Yes. Stripe will email a payment receipt after checkout is completed.

What is the refund deadline?

Full refunds are available when requested before the event date, October 24, 2026.
