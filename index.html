Frequently asked questions

Good to know

What does the ticket include?

The $45 ticket covers Saturday evening’s dinner and reunion celebration at DiBella’s. The main reunion begins at CDO at 5 PM with a tour and class pictures, followed by dinner at DiBella’s. Friday at Hotel Congress and Saturday’s noon family picnic are separate weekend activities.

Can I bring a guest?

Yes. Purchase one ticket per person attending the Saturday evening event and enter the full name of every guest during registration.

Will I receive confirmation?

Yes. Stripe will email a payment receipt after checkout is completed.

What is the refund deadline?

Full refunds are available when requested before the event date, October 24, 2026.

Where are we now?

Twenty-five years, so many different paths! Help us tell the story of the Class of 2001 by sharing a little about your life today. We’ll use your answers to put together fun class statistics for the reunion.

Answer once per CDO graduate, not per ticket. Answers are saved with your paid registration and available to reunion organizers. We will share aggregate statistics; written answers will only be shared with your permission. Please avoid sensitive personal details.

If two graduates share children, include them in only one response to avoid counting them twice. Statistics will describe classmates who responded, not the entire class.
